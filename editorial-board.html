Our editorial standards

Manuscripts must be original works of satire, written in the style of serious academic research. Implausible hypotheses are encouraged; claims that the work is genuine research are not. Every publication carries a clear fiction notice. No actual DOI, ISSN, accreditation or peer-review certification is claimed.
