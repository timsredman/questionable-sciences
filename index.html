The Development of a Social Media Enabled Yucca Plant

An Experimental Investigation into Autonomous Botanical Communication on X (Formerly Twitter)

Professor I. M. Barking · Dr Leaf N. Alone · Dr Hugh Jass · Professor Y. U. Cca

International Institute of Unnecessary Scientific Advancement, University of Lower Twatton (fictional)

Abstract

This entirely fictional six-week experiment connected Kevin, a yucca plant, to moisture sensors, a Raspberry Pi, an AI text generator and the social media platform X. Instead of providing useful horticultural observations, Kevin allegedly developed an increasingly aggressive online personality, accumulated 34,619 followers and raised a difficult question: is Kevin simply a bit of a prick?

The study's invented findings provide no legitimate evidence of plant intelligence, social media competence or the existence of a research grant.

ABOUT JQS

Peer reviewed by people of questionable competence.

The Journal of Questionable Sciences is a creative comedy project. Its papers imitate the conventions of scholarly publishing to explore absurd premises and impossible findings.

Everything here is satire. Authors, institutions, experiments, results, editorial decisions, grants and academic credentials may be invented. The journal is not accredited, indexed or genuinely peer reviewed. It does not offer medical, scientific or professional advice.
